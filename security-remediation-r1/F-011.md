## 实现方式

- 建立并导出显式 server-side `appointmentTransitionTable`。
- 状态：`NONE`, `DRAFT`, `PUBLISHED`, `WITHDRAWN`, `COMPLETED`, `CANCELLED`。
- 动作：`saveDraft`, `publish`, `withdraw`, `complete`, `cancel`。
- `COMPLETED` 与 `CANCELLED` 没有任何普通后继状态；所有 action 均在写入前返回 typed 409。
- `saveAppointmentDraft`, `publishAppointment`, `withdrawAppointment`, `completeAppointment`, `cancelAppointment` 均复用同一 transition assertion。

允许矩阵：

- `NONE`: save draft
- `DRAFT`: save draft, publish, cancel
- `PUBLISHED`: withdraw, complete, cancel
- `WITHDRAWN`: save draft, publish, cancel
- `COMPLETED`: none
- `CANCELLED`: none

## 新增测试

- 6 states × 5 actions = 30 个真实 service/SQLite transition cases。
- 10 个允许动作断言最终状态。
- 20 个非法动作断言 409，并对完整序列化快照验证：appointment row、position、version、timestamp、application、audit 全部零变化。
